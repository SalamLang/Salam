Brand and design system

Everything that makes Salam look like Salam: the logo in every approved color and format, the color ramps, type scale, spacing, shape and the syntax-highlight palette this site is built from.

Logo

The Salam mark is a four-petal flower built from four mirrored arcs — a nod to the Persian گل (flower) motif and to compass points. It ships in three colorways; pick whichever keeps the highest contrast against the background.

On light
On dark
Orange, on light
App icon
macOS icon
Do
  • Keep clear space of at least one petal-width around the mark
  • Use the boxed version at small sizes, such as app icons and favicons
  • Place it on high-contrast backgrounds: white, near-black, or brand orange
Don't
  • Recolor the mark outside the three approved colorways
  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the logo off-axis
  • Place it on busy photography without a solid backing shape

import encoding.uuid

func main:
    id := uuid.New()
    println uuid.String(id).Len(), uuid.Version(id), uuid.Variant(id) == uuid.VARIANT_RFC
    text := uuid.NewString()
    println uuid.IsValid(text), text.Len()
    println uuid.Nil()
    println uuid.Max()
    println uuid.IsNil(uuid.Nil()), uuid.IsNil(id)
end
