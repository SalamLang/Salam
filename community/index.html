The story of Salam

Salam is the Persian word for peace, and for hello. The language was designed by Seyyed Ali Mohammadiyeh and the Salam team, and grew out of more than a decade of language design work going back to 2015. The goal is to make programming more approachable for Persian speakers without giving up anything a systems language is supposed to offer.

Salam is released under the GNU GPL version 3: you are free to use it, read it, change it and redistribute it.
