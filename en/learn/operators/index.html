Operators

Operators work on values: they do arithmetic, compare things and join conditions together.

In this lesson
  1. Arithmetic
  2. Comparison and logic
  3. The ternary and compound operators
  4. Bitwise operators

Arithmetic

+ - * / % behave as they do in mathematics, and ** raises to a power. Multiplication and division happen before addition and subtraction, and parentheses change the order.

ops-arith.salam
func main:
    a := 17
    b := 5
    println a + b, a - b, a * b, a / b, a % b
    println 2 ** 10
    println (2 + 3) * 4, 2 + 3 * 4
end
Output22 12 85 3 2 1024 20 14

Comparison and logic

A comparison produces a bool. The operators are ==, !=, <, >, <= and >=. Join conditions with and and or, and invert one with not.

ops-compare.salam
func main:
    age := 20
    println age > 18, age < 18, age == 20, age != 20
    println age == 20, age != 30
    println age > 18, age <= 20
    println age >= 18 and age <= 60
    println age < 10 or age > 15
    println not (age > 18)
end
Outputtrue false true false true true true true true true false

The ternary and compound operators

condition ? a : b gives a when the condition holds and b otherwise. +=, -=, *=, /=, ++ and -- are shorthand for changing a variable in place.

ops-ternary.salam
func main:
    temperature := 32
    weather := temperature > 30 ? "hot" : "mild"
    println "the weather is", weather
    mut score := 10
    score *= 3
    score -= 5
    score--
    println score
end
Outputthe weather is hot 24

Bitwise operators

&, |, ^, ~ and the shifts << and >> work on the bits of a whole number.

ops-bitwise.salam
func main:
    a := 0b1100
    b := 0b1010
    println a & b, a | b, a ^ b
    println 1 << 4, 256 >> 2, ~0
end
Output8 14 6 16 64 -1

Two plus or minus signs in a row, as in a - -b, is an error; put the inner expression in parentheses: a - (-b).
