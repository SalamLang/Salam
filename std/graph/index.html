graph

Graph theory: traversal, shortest paths, spanning trees, flow, coloring, communities and centrality.

import graph
On this page
  1. Types
  2. Constants and variables
  3. Functions

This package has more than two hundred graph algorithms. Make a graph with NewUndirected or NewDirected, add edges with add_edge and add_weighted, then call build; or read the whole thing from text in one go with FromEdgeList. Nodes are numbered from zero to n minus one.

Everything from traversal and components to shortest paths, spanning trees, flow and cuts, matching, coloring, communities and centrality is ready to use. You can export the result with ToDot or ToMermaid to draw it. The algorithms that solve hard problems, such as maximum clique and Hamiltonian cycle, are meant for small graphs.
