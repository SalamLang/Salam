community.story_title = The story of Salam
community.story_text = Salam is the Persian word for peace, and for hello. The language was designed by Seyyed Ali Mohammadiyeh and the Salam team, and grew out of more than a decade of language design work going back to 2015. The goal is to make programming more approachable for Persian speakers without giving up anything a systems language is supposed to offer.
