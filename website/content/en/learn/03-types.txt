<h2 id="numbers">Number sizes and bases</h2>
<p>Numbers can be written in base sixteen (<code>0xFF</code>), base two (<code>0b1010</code>) and base eight (<code>0o17</code>), and you can group the digits with <code>_</code> to make them readable.</p>
{{example.types-ints}}
<p><code>sizeof</code> gives the size of a type in bytes:</p>
{{example.types-sizeof}}

<h2 id="cast">Converting between types</h2>
<p>Salam never converts between types behind your back; write the conversion with <code>as</code>. Dividing two whole numbers gives a whole number, with the fraction dropped. <code>to_int()</code> turns text into a number.</p>
