<h2 id="arith">Arithmetic</h2>
<p><code>+ - * / %</code> behave as they do in mathematics, and <code>**</code> raises to a power. Multiplication and division happen before addition and subtraction, and parentheses change the order.</p>
{{example.ops-arith}}

<h2 id="compare">Comparison and logic</h2>
<p>A comparison produces a <code>bool</code>. The operators are <code>==</code>, <code>!=</code>, <code>&lt;</code>, <code>&gt;</code>, <code>&lt;=</code> and <code>&gt;=</code>. Join conditions with <code>and</code> and <code>or</code>, and invert one with <code>not</code>.</p>
{{example.ops-compare}}

<h2 id="ternary">The ternary and compound operators</h2>
<p><code>condition ? a : b</code> gives <code>a</code> when the condition holds and <code>b</code> otherwise. <code>+=</code>, <code>-=</code>, <code>*=</code>, <code>/=</code>, <code>++</code> and <code>--</code> are shorthand for changing a variable in place.</p>
{{example.ops-ternary}}

<h2 id="bitwise">Bitwise operators</h2>
<p><code>&amp;</code>, <code>|</code>, <code>^</code>, <code>~</code> and the shifts <code>&lt;&lt;</code> and <code>&gt;&gt;</code> work on the bits of a whole number.</p>
{{example.ops-bitwise}}
<div class="note"><p>Two plus or minus signs in a row, as in <code>a - -b</code>, is an error; put the inner expression in parentheses: <code>a - (-b)</code>.</p></div>
>>
