<h2 id="if">if, else if, else</h2>
<p>The body of an <code>if</code> runs only when its condition holds. Test further conditions with <code>else if</code>, and cover everything that is left with <code>else</code>.</p>
{{example.conditions}}

<h2 id="inline">Short conditions, and joining them</h2>
<p>A short condition fits on one line. Join several conditions with <code>and</code> and <code>or</code>.</p>
{{example.cond-inline}}

<h2 id="rules">The rules a condition follows</h2>
<ul>
<li>A condition has to be a <code>bool</code>. <code>if number:</code> is an error; write <code>if number != 0:</code>.</li>
<li>A condition that is always true, or always false, is rejected, because one of the branches would never run.</li>
<li>No branch may be empty. Instead of an empty branch, invert the condition with <code>not</code>.</li>
</ul>
