<h2 id="repeat">repeat</h2>
<p><code>repeat 3:</code> runs the body three times. Add <code>in i</code> to get a counter called <code>i</code> that starts at zero. <code>repeat 1 to 10</code> walks a range with both ends included, <code>by 3</code> sets the step, and if the start is greater than the end the loop counts down.</p>
{{example.loop-count}}

<h2 id="until">The until loop</h2>
<p><code>until condition:</code> repeats for as long as the condition holds, so write the condition the way you want the loop to carry on.</p>
{{example.loops}}

<h2 id="each">each ... in</h2>
<p><code>each item in list</code> walks every element of an array, a vector or a map. The form <code>each index, item in list</code> gives you the position as well.</p>
{{example.loop-each}}

<h2 id="break">break and continue</h2>
<p><code>break</code> leaves the loop and <code>continue</code> jumps to the next turn. Inside nested loops, <code>break 2</code> leaves two loops at once.</p>
{{example.loop-break}}
