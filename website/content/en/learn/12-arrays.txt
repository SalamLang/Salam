<h2 id="basics">Building and reading an array</h2>
<p>Build an array with square brackets. Positions start at zero and <code>len()</code> gives the number of elements. To change elements, declare the array <code>mut</code>. Reading past the end is an error, caught before the program runs where it can be and at run time otherwise.</p>

<h2 id="slices">Slices</h2>
<p><code>array[start: end]</code> makes a slice over the elements from <code>start</code> up to but not including <code>end</code>. It is not a copy: it points at the same data. <code>[: 2]</code> takes from the beginning and <code>[4:]</code> runs to the end. A parameter of type <code>int[]</code> accepts any array or slice of ints, whatever its length.</p>
