<h2 id="alias">Aliases and distinct types</h2>
<p><code>type Id = int</code> is only another name for <code>int</code> and mixes freely with it. <code>type Meter: int</code>, on the other hand, builds a new type on top of <code>int</code> that cannot be confused with a plain int, and that can carry its own methods and operators.</p>
{{example.type-alias}}

<h2 id="operators">Operator overloading</h2>
<p>A method whose name is the operator itself, such as <code>func +(other: Vec): Vec</code>, defines that operator for the struct. The operators you can define are <code>+ - * / % ** == != &lt; &gt; &lt;= &gt;= not [] []=</code>.</p>
{{example.operator}}

<h2 id="variant">Variant</h2>
<p><code>Variant&lt;int, str&gt;</code> is a value that may be any one of several types. Match on the type names to get the value back with its real type.</p>
{{example.variant}}
