<h2 id="spawn">spawn and join</h2>
<p><code>spawn(function)</code> starts the function on a new thread and gives back its handle; <code>join(handle)</code> waits for that thread to finish. The function may take no parameters, or exactly one pointer: <code>spawn(function, data)</code>.</p>
