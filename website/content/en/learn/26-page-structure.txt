<h2 id="landmarks">Boxes and landmarks</h2>
<p><code>box</code> and <code>span</code> are the generic containers. A <code>box</code> is a block, it starts on a new line and takes the full width. A <code>span</code> sits inside a line of text. Neither one says anything about its content, so they are the right tools when the only reason for the container is styling.</p>
<p>When a part of the page has a job, there is an element that names it. <code>header</code> holds the introduction, <code>nav</code> a group of navigation links, <code>main</code> the content the page is about, <code>aside</code> something related but separate, and <code>footer</code> the closing part. <code>section</code> is a themed group of content that usually starts with a heading, <code>article</code> is a piece that makes sense alone, such as a post or a comment, and <code>search</code> wraps the controls for searching. Browsers draw all of them like a plain box. The difference is for the people who cannot see the page: a screen reader lists these parts as <em>landmarks</em> and lets its user jump to the main content or to the navigation in one key press. Search engines read the outline in the same way.</p>
{{example.web-structure-landmarks}}
<p>The example puts a <code>header</code> and a <code>nav</code> above, then a <code>main</code> with two sections and an <code>aside</code> next to it, then a <code>footer</code>. The <code>id</code> on each section is what the links <code>#soil</code> and <code>#water</code> point to: a link that starts with <code>#</code> scrolls to the element with that id. The <code>display = flex</code> and <code>gap</code> lines are CSS and are explained in the CSS lessons.</p>
<div class="note"><p>A page can have only one <code>main</code>, and it cannot sit inside an <code>article</code>. The compiler reports an error for a second one (E120). It also refuses a <code>header</code> or a <code>footer</code> inside another <code>header</code> or <code>footer</code>, and an <code>address</code> inside an <code>address</code>. A <code>header</code> or <code>footer</code> inside an <code>article</code> or a <code>section</code> is fine: it then belongs to that part and not to the whole page. When a page has two <code>nav</code> elements, tell them apart with <code>aria label</code>, for example <code>aria label = "Main"</code>, so that a screen reader can announce which one it is on.</p></div>

<h2 id="lists">Lists</h2>
<p><code>list</code> is a bullet list. <code>ordered list</code> is a numbered one, for steps and rankings where the order matters. Only <code>item</code> elements can sit directly inside a list, and an <code>item</code> holds the text of the entry in its <code>content</code>. To nest a list, put it inside an <code>item</code>, after the <code>content</code>, as the example does under Olives. Browsers use a different marker for each level.</p>
{{example.web-structure-lists}}
<p>An ordered list has three attributes. <code>reversed</code> counts down, so a list of three items is numbered 3, 2, 1. <code>start</code> is the number of the first item, and it can be zero or negative. <code>type</code> chooses the kind of marker: <code>1</code>, <code>a</code>, <code>A</code>, <code>i</code> or <code>I</code>. On a single <code>item</code>, <code>value</code> sets that item's number and the items after it carry on from there, which is why the last chapter above is IX. A bullet list ignores all of these. The marker can also be changed with the CSS property <code>list style type</code>, for example <code>persian</code> for Persian digits.</p>
<p><code>menu</code> is a list of commands, for example the buttons of a toolbar. It holds <code>item</code> elements like <code>list</code> does. Do not use it for the links of a site: that is a <code>nav</code> with a <code>list</code> inside.</p>

<h2 id="definitions">Description lists</h2>
<p>A <code>description list</code> is for name and value pairs: a glossary, the details of a product, the facts in a profile. A <code>term</code> is the name, and the <code>description</code> after it is the value. One term can have several descriptions, as the Footer entry in the first list shows, and several terms can share one description. Wrap each group in a <code>box</code> when you want to style a group as a unit, or to keep the groups visibly apart, as in the second list.</p>
{{example.web-structure-definitions}}
<p>You always write <code>term</code> and <code>description</code>. The compiler tells the two situations apart by the parent: directly inside the list they are the plain elements, and inside a <code>box</code> that sits directly inside a list they are the variants called <code>term in box</code> and <code>description in box</code>. They produce the same tags. Outside a description list neither is allowed, and the compiler says so.</p>

<h2 id="disclosure">Details and summary</h2>
<p><code>details</code> is a block the reader can open and close, with no script at all. Its first child must be a <code>summary</code>, and the compiler refuses it anywhere else. The summary is the line that is always visible and acts as the button. Everything after the summary is hidden until the block is opened. With <code>open = true</code> a block starts open. Give several blocks the same <code>name</code> and they become an exclusive group: opening one closes the others, which is exactly an accordion. A block without a name stays independent.</p>
{{example.web-structure-details}}
<p>In the example the first two blocks share the name <code>faq</code>, and the third has no name. Click them to see the difference. The <code>global</code> lines at the top only style the boxes and the pointer. They use plain tag names as selectors.</p>

<h2 id="dialog">Dialog</h2>
<p><code>dialog</code> is a box that floats above the page, for a message or a question. It has two ways of appearing. With <code>open = true</code> it is shown at once, with no dimming of the page behind it. To show it as a <em>modal</em>, a dialog that dims the page, traps the keyboard focus and closes on the Esc key, you have to ask the browser from a script. In Salam that is one call from a click handler: <code>dom.Call0(dom.ById("saved"), "showModal")</code>, and <code>"close"</code> in the same way to close it. The attribute <code>closedby</code> says what else may close it: <code>closerequest</code> (the Esc key and the device's close gesture), <code>any</code> (Esc or a click outside) or <code>none</code> (only your own code).</p>
{{example.web-structure-dialog}}
<p>The page starts with <code>import dom</code>, which is needed for the calls. <code>dom.ById</code> finds the element by its id, and the compiler checks that an element with that id exists in the same page. <code>dom.SetText</code> changes the text of the paragraph. Events and the <code>dom</code> package get their own lessons later.</p>

<h2 id="figures">Figures and addresses</h2>
<p><code>figure</code> wraps something that belongs with the text but can stand on its own: a picture, a chart, a listing. The <code>figure caption</code> inside it names the figure, and a screen reader connects the two. The image gets its own text description in the <code>alt</code> attribute, and the next lesson explains why that is a different thing from the caption. <code>address</code> is for contact details of the author of the nearest article or of the whole page, not for any postal address you happen to mention. Browsers set it in italic, and the example turns that off with <code>font style = normal</code>.</p>
{{example.web-structure-figure}}

<h2 id="blog">A whole page</h2>
<p>Here is a blog post that uses most of the elements above. The page <code>header</code> holds the name of the site and a <code>search</code> with its form. A <code>nav</code> follows. The <code>main</code> holds one <code>article</code>, which has a <code>header</code> of its own with the title and the date in a <code>time</code> element, and two <code>section</code> parts that each start with a level 2 heading. An <code>aside</code> next to it lists related reading, and the page <code>footer</code> holds the <code>address</code>. Read the code from the outside in, and notice that every element has a reason to be there.</p>
{{example.web-structure-blog}}

<h2 id="reference">Reference: structure and list elements</h2>
<p>Every element of this lesson is below, with its attributes and a short example. The examples are checked by the compiler, so you can paste any of them inside a <code>page:</code> block. The attributes that every element has, such as <code>id</code> and <code>title</code>, are in <a href="/learn/page-head/">the lesson on the head and global attributes</a>.</p>
<h3 id="ref-landmarks">Containers and landmarks</h3>
<h3 id="el-box"><code>box</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>جعبه</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;div&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A generic block container (HTML <code>div</code>). It starts on a new line and takes the full width. It has no meaning of its own, so use it only to group things for layout or style when no other element fits.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>box: padding = 12 background = &quot;#f1f5f9&quot; border radius = 8
    paragraph: content = &quot;Two paragraphs share one box.&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;The box gives them a background.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-section"><code>section</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>بخش</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;section&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A themed group of content, almost always with a heading at its start. A page can have many sections; give each a heading so the outline makes sense.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>section:
    heading: size = 2 content = &quot;Watering&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;Water early in the morning.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-article"><code>article</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>مقاله</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;article&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A self-contained piece that would make sense if it were copied somewhere else: a blog post, a news story, a product card, a comment. Articles can be nested, for example comments inside a post.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>article:
    heading: size = 2 content = &quot;Mulch matters&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;A layer of straw keeps the soil damp.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-aside"><code>aside</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>کناری</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;aside&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Content that is related to the main content but could be removed without breaking it: a sidebar, a note, a pull quote. Screen readers announce it as a complementary landmark.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>aside: padding = 12 background = &quot;#fef9c3&quot; border radius = 8
    strong: content = &quot;Tip&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;Water the roots, not the leaves.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-header"><code>header</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>سرصفحه</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;header&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The introduction of the page or of a section: a title, a logo, a menu. Directly in the page it is the banner landmark. Inside an <code>article</code> or <code>section</code> it introduces only that part. It cannot be placed inside another <code>header</code> or <code>footer</code>.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>header: background = &quot;#0f766e&quot; color = &quot;white&quot; padding = 12
    h1: content = &quot;Garden Notes&quot; margin = 0 end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-footer"><code>footer</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پاورقی</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;footer&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The closing part of the page or of a section: author, copyright, related links. Directly in the page it is the content information landmark. It cannot be placed inside a <code>header</code> or another <code>footer</code>.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>footer: padding = 12 border top = &quot;1px solid #cbd5e1&quot; color = &quot;#475569&quot;
    small: content = &quot;Written by hand in Salam.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-nav"><code>nav</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>ناوبری</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;nav&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A group of links for moving around the site or the page. Not every group of links is navigation, only the main ones. When a page has several, tell them apart with <code>aria label</code>.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>nav: aria label = &quot;Main&quot; display = flex gap = 16
    link: url = &quot;#&quot; content = &quot;Home&quot; end
    link: url = &quot;#&quot; content = &quot;Archive&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-main"><code>main</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>اصلی</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;main&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The content the page exists for. A page can have only one, and it cannot be inside an <code>article</code>, <code>aside</code>, <code>header</code>, <code>footer</code> or <code>nav</code>. A screen reader can jump straight to it.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>main: max width = 600
    heading: size = 1 content = &quot;Why mulch matters&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;The page content goes here.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-search"><code>search</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>جستجو</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;search&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Wraps the controls used to search the page or the site. It is a landmark of its own, so a screen reader can jump to the search box. Put a form inside it.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>search:
    form: input: type = &quot;search&quot; placeholder = &quot;Search&quot; aria label = &quot;Search the blog&quot; end end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-address"><code>address</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>آدرس</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;address&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Contact information for the author of the nearest <code>article</code> or of the whole page. It is not for every postal address in a text. Browsers show it in italic; <code>font style = normal</code> turns that off. It cannot contain another <code>address</code>.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>address: font style = normal
    content = &quot;Garden Notes&quot;
    break: end
    link: url = &quot;mailto:hello@example.com&quot; content = &quot;hello@example.com&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="ref-lists">Lists</h3>
<h3 id="el-list"><code>list</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>فهرست</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;ul&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A bullet list (HTML <code>ul</code>). Use it when the order of the entries does not matter. Only <code>item</code> elements can be its direct children.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>list:
    item: content = &quot;Bread&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Olives&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Tea&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-ordered-list"><code>ordered list</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>فهرست شماره‌دار</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;ol&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A numbered list (HTML <code>ol</code>) for steps and rankings, where the order matters. Its attributes change the counting and the look of the numbers.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>ordered list:
    item: content = &quot;Mix the dough&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Let it rise&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Bake&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>What it does</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr id="at-ordered-list-reversed"><td><code>reversed</code><br><span class="ref-alt">معکوس</span></td><td>A boolean. With <code>true</code> the list counts down, so three items are numbered 3, 2, 1. The items stay in the order you wrote them. <small class="ref-accepts">[bool]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">ordered list: reversed = true
    item: content = &quot;Third place&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Second place&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Winner&quot; end
end</code></td></tr>
<tr id="at-ordered-list-start"><td><code>start</code><br><span class="ref-alt">شروع</span></td><td>A whole number, the number of the first item. It may be zero or negative. The default is 1 (or the last number when the list is reversed). <small class="ref-accepts">[int]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">ordered list: start = 5
    item: content = &quot;Fifth&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Sixth&quot; end
end</code></td></tr>
<tr id="at-ordered-list-type"><td><code>type</code><br><span class="ref-alt">نوع</span></td><td>The kind of marker: <code>1</code> for numbers (the default), <code>a</code> or <code>A</code> for letters, <code>i</code> or <code>I</code> for Roman numerals in lower or upper case. <small class="ref-accepts">[enum: ol_type]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">ordered list: type = &quot;A&quot;
    item: content = &quot;Apples&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Pears&quot; end
end</code></td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="el-item"><code>item</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>مورد</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;li&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>One entry of a <code>list</code>, an <code>ordered list</code> or a <code>menu</code> (HTML <code>li</code>). Its text goes in <code>content</code>; a nested list goes after it.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>list:
    item:
        content = &quot;Olives&quot;
        list:
            item: content = &quot;Green&quot; end
            item: content = &quot;Black&quot; end
        end
    end
end</code></pre>
<p class="ref-meta">Only inside: <code>list</code>, <code>ordered list</code>, <code>menu</code></p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>What it does</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr id="at-item-value"><td><code>value</code><br><span class="ref-alt">مقدار</span></td><td>A whole number. In an ordered list it sets the number of this item, and the items after it continue from there. In a bullet list it has no effect. <small class="ref-accepts">[int]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">ordered list:
    item: content = &quot;One&quot; end
    item: value = 10 content = &quot;Ten&quot; end
    item: content = &quot;Eleven&quot; end
end</code></td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="el-menu"><code>menu</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>منو</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;menu&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A list of commands, such as the buttons of a toolbar (HTML <code>menu</code>). It holds <code>item</code> elements like a <code>list</code>. Site navigation is a <code>nav</code> with a <code>list</code>, not a <code>menu</code>.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>menu:
    item: button: content = &quot;Cut&quot; end end
    item: button: content = &quot;Copy&quot; end end
    item: button: content = &quot;Paste&quot; end end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="ref-definitions">Description lists</h3>
<h3 id="el-description-list"><code>description list</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>فهرست تعریف</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;dl&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A list of name and value groups (HTML <code>dl</code>): a glossary, the facts about a product. Its children are <code>term</code> and <code>description</code> elements, or <code>box</code> elements that each wrap one group.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>description list:
    term: content = &quot;Salam&quot; end
    description: content = &quot;A programming language.&quot; end
    term: content = &quot;Page&quot; end
    description: content = &quot;What the browser shows.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-term"><code>term</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>واژه</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;dt&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The name part of a group (HTML <code>dt</code>). It may be followed by one or more <code>description</code> elements. It is allowed directly inside a <code>description list</code>, and inside a <code>box</code> there.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>description list:
    term: content = &quot;Colour&quot; end
    description: content = &quot;Teal&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<p class="ref-meta">Only inside: <code>description list</code></p>
<h3 id="el-description"><code>description</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>شرح</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;dd&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The value part of a group (HTML <code>dd</code>). A term can have several descriptions. It is allowed directly inside a <code>description list</code>, and inside a <code>box</code> there.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>description list:
    term: content = &quot;Sizes&quot; end
    description: content = &quot;Small&quot; end
    description: content = &quot;Large&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<p class="ref-meta">Only inside: <code>description list</code></p>
<h3 id="el-term-in-box"><code>term in box</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>واژه</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;dt&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The same tag as <code>term</code>, used when the name sits inside a <code>box</code> that wraps one group of a <code>description list</code>. You write <code>term</code>; the compiler picks this variant from the parent. It is refused anywhere else.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>description list:
    box:
        term: content = &quot;Kind&quot; end
        description: content = &quot;Language&quot; end
    end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-description-in-box"><code>description in box</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>شرح</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;dd&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The same tag as <code>description</code>, used inside a <code>box</code> that wraps one group of a <code>description list</code>. You write <code>description</code>; the compiler picks this variant from the parent.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>description list:
    box:
        term: content = &quot;Born&quot; end
        description: content = &quot;2018&quot; end
        description: content = &quot;Tehran&quot; end
    end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="ref-widgets">Details, dialog and figures</h3>
<h3 id="el-details"><code>details</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>جزئیات</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;details&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A block the reader can open and close without any script. Its first child must be a <code>summary</code>, and the rest is hidden until the block is open.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>details:
    summary: content = &quot;More about mulch&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;Straw, bark or leaves all work.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>What it does</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr id="at-details-name"><td><code>name</code><br><span class="ref-alt">نام</span></td><td>A string that makes <code>details</code> blocks with the same name an exclusive group: opening one closes the others. Blocks without a name do not affect each other. <small class="ref-accepts">[string]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">details: name = &quot;tips&quot;
    summary: content = &quot;Spring&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;Sow seeds.&quot; end
end
details: name = &quot;tips&quot;
    summary: content = &quot;Autumn&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;Plant bulbs.&quot; end
end</code></td></tr>
<tr id="at-details-open"><td><code>open</code><br><span class="ref-alt">باز</span></td><td>A boolean. With <code>true</code> the block is open when the page loads. The reader can still close it. <small class="ref-accepts">[bool]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">details: open = true
    summary: content = &quot;Read this first&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;It starts open.&quot; end
end</code></td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="el-summary"><code>summary</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>خلاصه</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;summary&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The always visible line of a <code>details</code> block; it works as the button that opens and closes it. It must be the first child of <code>details</code>.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>details:
    summary: content = &quot;Shipping&quot; font weight = &quot;bold&quot; end
    paragraph: content = &quot;Orders leave on Monday.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<p class="ref-meta">Only inside: <code>details</code></p>
<h3 id="el-dialog"><code>dialog</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پنجره گفتگو</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;dialog&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A box that floats above the page, for a message or a question. With <code>open = true</code> it is shown at once. To show it as a modal call <code>showModal</code> on it from a click handler, and <code>close</code> to hide it.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>dialog: open = true padding = 16 border radius = 8
    paragraph: content = &quot;Shown without a script.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>What it does</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr id="at-dialog-open"><td><code>open</code><br><span class="ref-alt">باز</span></td><td>A boolean. With <code>true</code> the dialog is visible when the page loads, as a normal, non-modal box with no dimmed backdrop. <small class="ref-accepts">[bool]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">dialog: open = true
    paragraph: content = &quot;I am open.&quot; end
end</code></td></tr>
<tr id="at-dialog-closedby"><td><code>closedby</code><br><span class="ref-alt">بستن با</span></td><td>What may close a modal dialog besides your own code: <code>closerequest</code> (the Esc key and the device's close gesture), <code>any</code> (a close request or a click outside the dialog) or <code>none</code> (only code). <small class="ref-accepts">[enum: closed_by]</small></td><td><code class="snip" dir="ltr">dialog: closedby = &quot;any&quot;
    paragraph: content = &quot;Click outside to close.&quot; end

</tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="el-figure"><code>figure</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>شکل</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;figure&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Content that goes with the text but can stand on its own: an image, a chart, a code listing. It may hold one <code>figure caption</code>.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>figure:
    preformatted: content = &quot;1 + 1 = 2&quot; end
    figure caption: content = &quot;Listing 1. A sum.&quot; end
end</code></pre>
<h3 id="el-figure-caption"><code>figure caption</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>زیرنویس شکل</code>; HTML: <code>&lt;figcaption&gt;</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The caption of a <code>figure</code> (HTML <code>figcaption</code>). A figure can have only one.</p>
<pre class="code snip" dir="ltr"><code>figure:
    paragraph: content = &quot;A chart would be here.&quot; end
    figure caption: content = &quot;Figure 2. Rain per month.&quot; font size = &quot;0.9rem&quot; end
end</code></pre>
