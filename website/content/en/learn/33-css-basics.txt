<h2 id="properties">Properties are attributes</h2>
<p>A style property is written like any attribute: <code>name = value</code> inside the element. Names may have spaces, such as <code>min width</code> or <code>font size</code>. A misspelled name or a value of the wrong kind is a compile error, and the message tells you what was expected. Every property of the language is listed in the <a href="/learn/style-reference/">style vocabulary reference</a>.</p>
<h2 id="values">Numbers, percent and units</h2>
<p>A bare number uses the natural unit of the property: pixels for lengths, milliseconds for times, degrees for angles. A percent is a share of the parent. Other units are written after the number: <code>em</code>, <code>rem</code>, <code>vw</code>, <code>vh</code>, <code>fr</code>, <code>ms</code>, <code>s</code>, <code>deg</code>. A comma continues one property, so <code>padding = 8, 16</code> is 8 above and below and 16 at the sides.</p>
{{example.dsl-values}}
<h2 id="colors">Colours</h2>
<p>A colour is written in quotes: a name such as <code>"red"</code>, or a hex colour with 3, 4, 6 or 8 digits (<code>"#rgb"</code>, <code>"#rrggbb"</code>, <code>"#rrggbbaa"</code>), where the last pair is the transparency. The <a href="/learn/style-reference/">reference</a> lists the other colour forms of the standard.</p>
{{example.dsl-colors}}
<h2 id="box">The box</h2>
<p>Every box has content, then <code>padding</code>, then a <code>border</code>, then <code>margin</code>. Sizes always include the padding and the border, so a box with <code>width = 320</code> is 320 wide whatever its padding. Padding and margin take one to four values (top, end, bottom, start) or a single side such as <code>padding top</code>, <code>margin x</code> and <code>padding y</code>. A margin can be <code>auto</code>.</p>
{{example.dsl-box}}
<h2 id="sizes">Sizes</h2>
<p><code>width</code> and <code>height</code> take a length, <code>auto</code>, <code>fill</code> (the whole parent) or <code>fit</code> (just the content). <code>min width</code>, <code>max width</code> and the height versions limit them, and <code>aspect ratio</code> derives one side from the other.</p>
{{example.dsl-sizes}}
<h2 id="overflow">Content that does not fit</h2>
<p><code>overflow</code> is <code>visible</code>, <code>hidden</code>, <code>scroll</code> or <code>auto</code>, with <code>overflow x</code> and <code>overflow y</code> for one direction. <code>clip = true</code> cuts children at the rounded corners. <code>visible = false</code> removes an element from the layout.</p>
{{example.dsl-overflow}}
<h2 id="reuse">Components</h2>
<p>A <code>component</code> packages a piece of page with parameters, and <code>content: end</code> marks where its children go.</p>
