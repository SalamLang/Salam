<h2 id="basics">Font, size and colour</h2>
<p><code>font</code> takes a list of families; the words <code>sans</code>, <code>serif</code>, <code>mono</code> and <code>system</code> name the generic ones, and the first family that exists is used. <code>font size</code> is a length, <code>font weight</code> is a word (<code>light</code>, <code>normal</code>, <code>bold</code>, <code>black</code>) or a number from 100 to 900, and <code>italic</code> is true or false. <code>line height</code> is a number of times the font size.</p>

<h2 id="inherit">Inheritance</h2>
<p>Properties of text are passed down to children: <code>color</code>, <code>font</code>, <code>font size</code>, <code>font weight</code>, <code>italic</code>, <code>line height</code>, <code>letter spacing</code>, <code>text align</code> and the others in the text part of the <a href="/learn/style-reference/#text">reference</a>. Box properties such as <code>padding</code> are not inherited.</p>
<h2 id="align">Alignment and direction</h2>
<p><code>text align</code> is <code>start</code>, <code>center</code>, <code>end</code> or <code>justify</code>. Start and end follow the text direction, so the same page works in English and in Persian. <code>direction</code> is <code>auto</code>, <code>ltr</code> or <code>rtl</code>.</p>
<p>Without a setting the page is right to left when its <code>lang</code> is a right to left language (Persian, Arabic, Hebrew, Urdu and the like) or the source is written in Persian, and left to right otherwise. That default reaches every element, headings and input placeholders included. The <code>dir</code> attribute (<code>ltr</code>, <code>rtl</code> or <code>auto</code>) on the page or on any element, and the <code>direction</code> property, change it for that element and its children. Only <code>auto</code> looks at the text: the element takes the direction of its first strong letter, so Persian text is right to left and Latin text is left to right.</p>
<h2 id="clip">Cutting and shadows</h2>
<p><code>ellipsis = true</code> keeps one line and ends it with three dots. <code>max lines = 2</code> does the same for two lines. <code>white space = keep</code> keeps spaces and newlines. <code>transform text</code> changes the case, and <code>text shadow</code> takes x, y, blur and colour.</p>
