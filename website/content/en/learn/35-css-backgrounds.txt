<h2 id="paint">Paints</h2>
<p><code>background</code> takes a colour, and a hex colour with an alpha pair is partly transparent. The standard also has gradients and images as paints; see the <a href="/learn/style-reference/#look">reference</a>.</p>
{{example.dsl-background}}
<h2 id="image">Images as background</h2>
<p><code>background size</code> is <code>cover</code>, <code>contain</code>, <code>auto</code> or a length. <code>background position</code> takes words such as <code>center</code> or lengths, and <code>background repeat</code> is true, false, <code>x</code> or <code>y</code>.</p>
<h2 id="effects">Effects on the whole element</h2>
<p><code>opacity</code> goes from 0 to 1. The filters <code>brightness</code>, <code>contrast</code>, <code>grayscale</code>, <code>saturate</code>, <code>sepia</code>, <code>invert</code> and <code>hue rotate</code> change how the element is drawn, and the same names with <code>backdrop</code> in front change what shows behind it. <code>translate</code>, <code>rotate</code>, <code>scale</code> and <code>skew</code> move and bend an element after layout, so neighbours do not move.</p>
{{example.dsl-filters}}
