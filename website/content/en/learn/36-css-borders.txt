<h2 id="border">Border</h2>
<p><code>border</code> takes width, style and colour in any order: <code>border = 2, solid, "#3366aa"</code>. The styles are <code>solid</code>, <code>dashed</code>, <code>dotted</code>, <code>double</code> and <code>none</code>. One side has its own property (<code>border bottom</code>, <code>border start</code>), and the parts have theirs (<code>border width</code>, <code>border style</code>, <code>border color</code>).</p>
<h2 id="radius">Radius and outline</h2>
<p><code>radius</code> takes one to four lengths for the corners, or one corner such as <code>radius top start</code>. <code>outline</code> is written like a border but is drawn outside the box and takes no space; <code>outline offset</code> moves it away.</p>
{{example.dsl-borders}}
<h2 id="shadow">Shadows</h2>
<p><code>shadow</code> takes x, y, blur, spread and colour, and the word <code>inset</code> for an inner shadow. Square brackets group one shadow when you list several.</p>
