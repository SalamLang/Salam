lesson.body <<
<h2 id="layout">The five layouts</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>column</code>: children from top to bottom. This is the default.</li>
<li><code>row</code>: children along the text direction. In Persian it runs from right to left.</li>
<li><code>grid</code>: children in rows and columns that you define.</li>
<li><code>stack</code>: all children in the same place, later ones on top.</li>
<li><code>flow</code>: children like words in a paragraph. Text elements use it.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="gap">Gap, align and justify</h2>
<p><code>gap</code> is the space between children (one value, or row gap then column gap). <code>align</code> works across the direction of the layout (<code>start</code>, <code>center</code>, <code>end</code>, <code>stretch</code>, <code>baseline</code>) and <code>justify</code> along it (<code>start</code>, <code>center</code>, <code>end</code>, <code>between</code>, <code>around</code>, <code>evenly</code>).</p>
{{example.dsl-layout}}
<h2 id="grow">Sharing the free space</h2>
<p>A child with <code>grow = 1</code> takes a share of the space the others leave. <code>grow = 2</code> takes twice the share. <code>shrink</code> and <code>basis</code> tune the start size, and <code>align self</code> overrides <code>align</code> for one child.</p>
{{example.dsl-grow}}
<h2 id="grid">Grid</h2>
<p><code>columns</code> and <code>rows</code> list the tracks: a length, a share such as <code>1fr</code>, or <code>auto</code>. A child can use <code>column</code>, <code>row</code>, <code>column span</code> and <code>row span</code>.</p>
{{example.dsl-grid}}
<h2 id="stack">Stack and wrap</h2>
<p>A <code>stack</code> centres its children with <code>align</code> and <code>justify</code>. <code>wrap = true</code> lets a row or a column start a new line when it is full, and <code>reverse = true</code> reverses the order.</p>
{{example.dsl-stack}}
{{example.dsl-flow}}
<h2 id="end">Packing to the end</h2>
<p><code>end</code> is a value like any other word: <code>justify = "end"</code> packs the children at the end of the main axis, <code>align = "end"</code> at the end of the cross axis, and <code>text align = "end"</code> aligns text to the end of the line, which is the right edge in English and the left edge in Persian.</p>
{{example.dsl-align-end}}
<h2 id="position">Taking an element out of the flow</h2>
<p><code>position = "absolute"</code> with <code>top</code>, <code>start</code>, <code>end</code> or <code>bottom</code> places a child against its parent, and <code>layer</code> orders overlapping elements.</p>
