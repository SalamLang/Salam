<h2 id="sec-buttons">Buttons</h2>
<p>A page is a <code>page</code> block and gets this look unless you write <code>design = "none"</code>, which drops the look and keeps the behaviour. Variants and sizes are one word attributes, and their values are written in quotes.</p>
<h3 id="el-button"><code>button</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>دکمه</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A button runs code or sends a form. The <code>variant</code> attribute picks its look and <code>size</code> its size. Quote the value, as in <code>variant = "outline"</code>. A button with <code>disabled = true</code> is dimmed and ignores clicks.</p>

<h3 id="el-button-sizes"><code>button sizes</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>اندازه‌های دکمه</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The sizes are <code>xs</code>, <code>sm</code>, the default, and <code>lg</code>. The icon sizes <code>icon xs</code>, <code>icon sm</code>, <code>icon</code> and <code>icon lg</code> make a square button for one symbol.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-button-sizes}}
<h3 id="el-theme-toggle"><code>theme toggle</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>تغییر پوسته</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A button that flips the page between light and dark and remembers the choice. It takes the same <code>variant</code> and <code>size</code> as a button. Press it in the preview to see the page change.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-theme-toggle}}
<h2 id="sec-text">Text and choices</h2>
<h3 id="el-input-types"><code>input</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>ورودی</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A one line box. The <code>type</code> decides the keyboard and the checks, and the shadcn look is the same for all of them: <code>text</code>, <code>email</code>, <code>password</code>, <code>number</code>, <code>search</code>, <code>date</code> and the rest. <code>placeholder</code> is the grey hint, <code>disabled</code> dims the box, and the focus ring appears on keyboard focus. Checkbox, radio and range have their own looks, shown below.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-input-types}}
<h3 id="el-textarea"><code>text area</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>ناحیه متن</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A multi line box, written as an <code>input</code> with <code>type = "textarea"</code>. <code>rows</code> sets the starting height and the box grows with its content.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-textarea}}
<h3 id="el-label"><code>label</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>برچسب</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The text that names a control. Give the control an <code>id</code> and write it in the <code>for</code> of the label: a click on the label then focuses the control. A label next to a disabled control is dimmed too.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-label}}
<h3 id="el-checkbox"><code>checkbox</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>تیک</code>)</span></h3>
<p>An <code>input</code> with <code>type = "checkbox"</code>. <code>checked = true</code> starts it ticked and <code>disabled</code> dims it. <code>indeterminate = true</code> shows the in-between dash for a "select all" box; the first click on it clears the state, and a page script can set or remove the <code>data-indeterminate</code> attribute to change it later. The box also gets <code>aria-checked="mixed"</code> for screen readers.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-checkbox}}
{{example.ds-forms-checkbox-mixed}}
<h3 id="el-radio-group"><code>radio group</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>گروه رادیویی</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Several <code>input</code> elements with <code>type = "radio"</code> and the same <code>name</code>, held in a <code>radio group</code>. The group gives them even spacing and the radiogroup role for screen readers. Only one of them can be chosen.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-radio-group}}
<h3 id="el-select"><code>select</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>انتخاب</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The native <code>select</code> with <code>option</code> children, drawn with a border and a chevron. Because it is the real control, the picker, the keyboard and the form value all work as in any browser. <code>control size = "sm"</code> makes it 32 pixels high instead of 36. (<code>size</code> is already the row count of an HTML select, so the look has its own name.)</p>
{{example.ds-forms-select}}
{{example.ds-forms-select-small}}
<h3 id="el-invalid"><code>aria invalid</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>نامعتبر دسترسی‌پذیری</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Set <code>aria invalid = "true"</code> on an input, text area, select, checkbox or radio to mark its value as wrong. Screen readers announce it and the control gets the red border and soft red ring. Drive it from your own checks; leave it out when the value is fine.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-invalid}}
<h2 id="sec-layout">Fields and groups</h2>
<h3 id="el-field"><code>field</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>فیلد</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A label, a control, a description and an error laid out as one unit. <code>field set</code> and <code>legend</code> group fields, <code>field group</code> stacks them, and inside a <code>field</code> you can use <code>field label</code>, <code>field content</code>, <code>field title</code>, <code>field description</code>, <code>field error</code> and <code>field separator</code>.</p>

<p>An input fused with text or buttons at its edges. Children are the <code>input</code>, <code>input group addon</code> and <code>input group text</code>, and a <code>button</code>. The <code>align</code> attribute of the addon picks its side.</p>

<h3 id="el-button-group"><code>button group</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>گروه دکمه</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Buttons joined into one bar with shared borders. <code>orientation</code> is <code>horizontal</code> (default) or <code>vertical</code>. Inside you can also use <code>button group text</code> and <code>button group separator</code>.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-button-group}}
<h2 id="sec-toggles">Toggles and small controls</h2>
<h3 id="el-switch"><code>switch</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>کلید</code>)</span></h3>
<p>An on and off control. It sends its <code>value</code> under its <code>name</code> when on, and fires the <code>change</code> event on every flip. Attributes: <code>checked</code>, <code>name</code>, <code>value</code>, <code>disabled</code> and <code>size</code> (<code>default</code> or <code>sm</code>).</p>
{{example.ds-forms-switch}}
<h3 id="el-slider"><code>slider</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>لغزنده</code>)</span></h3>
<p>An <code>input</code> with <code>type = "range"</code>, with <code>min</code>, <code>max</code> and <code>value</code>. The filled part of the track follows the value as you drag. <code>orientation = "vertical"</code> stands it up, with the smallest value at the bottom.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-slider}}
<h3 id="el-range-slider"><code>range slider</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>لغزنده بازه</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A slider with two thumbs for a lower and an upper value. <code>min</code>, <code>max</code> and <code>step</code> set the scale, <code>low</code> and <code>high</code> the starting values, and <code>orientation</code> is <code>horizontal</code> or <code>vertical</code>. The thumbs cannot cross. The element carries <code>data-low</code>, <code>data-high</code> and <code>data-value</code> (as <code>"20,70"</code>), fires <code>ds-change</code> with <code>detail.low</code> and <code>detail.high</code>, and with a <code>name</code> sends two form values, <code>name-min</code> and <code>name-max</code>.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-slider-range}}
<h3 id="el-toggle"><code>toggle</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>تغییر وضعیت</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A button that stays pressed. <code>pressed = true</code> starts it on, <code>variant</code> is <code>default</code> or <code>outline</code>, <code>size</code> is <code>default</code>, <code>sm</code> or <code>lg</code>. It sets <code>aria-pressed</code> and fires <code>change</code>.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-toggle}}
<h3 id="el-toggle-group"><code>toggle group</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>گروه تغییر وضعیت</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Several toggles that work together. With <code>type = "single"</code> (default) one can be on, with <code>"multiple"</code> any number. <code>variant</code> and <code>size</code> pass down to the toggles, <code>spacing</code> from 0 to 4 separates them, and the arrow keys, Home and End move between them. The first group below is single and outlined, the second is multiple.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-toggle-group}}
<h3 id="el-kbd"><code>keyboard</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>صفحه کلید</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A key cap, for showing a shortcut. <code>keyboard group</code> puts several caps side by side.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-kbd}}
<h3 id="el-input-otp"><code>input otp</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>کد یکبار مصرف</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A row of boxes for a one time code. <code>length</code> is the number of digits (6 by default) and <code>group size</code> the digits per group, with a dash between groups; 0 means no groups. Typing moves forward, backspace moves back and a paste fills all the boxes. It fires <code>change</code> on each digit and <code>complete</code> when all are filled, and <code>name</code> carries the whole code in a form.</p>
{{example.ds-forms-input-otp}}
