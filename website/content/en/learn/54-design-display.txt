lesson.body <<
<p>All of these restyle only while the page has <code>design = "shadcn"</code>, which is the default. With <code>design = "none"</code> they still produce the same structure and the same accessibility attributes, just without the look. All the styles sit in <code>:where()</code>, so any style you write yourself wins without a fight.</p>
<h2 id="cards">Cards and badges</h2>
<h3 id="el-card"><code>card</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>کارت</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A framed block for a group of related content. It is made of parts: <code>card header</code> holds the <code>card title</code>, the <code>card description</code> and an optional <code>card action</code> that sits at the end of the header, then come <code>card content</code> and <code>card footer</code>. Every part is optional.</p>
{{example.ds-display-card}}
<p>Add <code>class = "border-b"</code> to the header, or <code>class = "border-t"</code> to the footer, for a divider line.</p>
<h3 id="el-badge"><code>badge</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>نشان</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A small label for a status, a count or a tag. With <code>url</code> it becomes a link and gets hover styles.</p>
{{example.ds-display-badge-variants}}

<h2 id="messages">Messages and dividers</h2>
<h3 id="el-alert"><code>alert</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>هشدار</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A callout that draws attention to a message. It has <code>role="alert"</code>, so screen readers announce it. The parts are <code>alert title</code>, one line, and <code>alert description</code>, in a muted colour. A direct <code>svg</code> child of the alert becomes its icon.</p>
{{example.ds-display-alert-variants}}
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>Values</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><code>variant</code></td><td><code>default</code>, <code>destructive</code></td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="el-separator"><code>separator</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>جداکننده</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A thin line that divides content. Use <code>orientation = "vertical"</code> between items of a row. The plain <code>line</code> element (HTML <code>hr</code>) gets the same look and keeps its margins.</p>
{{example.ds-display-separator}}

<h2 id="status">Progress and loading</h2>
<h3 id="el-progress"><code>progress</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پیشرفت</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A bar that shows how far a task has come. Give it <code>value</code> and <code>max</code>. Without a <code>value</code> the bar is indeterminate and plays an animation, which is right when you do not know how long it takes.</p>
{{example.ds-display-progress}}
<h3 id="el-meter"><code>meter</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>سنجه</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A gauge for a value inside a known range, such as disk use. It is not for progress. <code>low</code>, <code>high</code> and <code>optimum</code> split the range into zones, and the colour of the bar tells which zone the value is in. Here the best value is 100, so 90 is good, 50 is so so and 15 is bad.</p>
{{example.ds-display-meter}}
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><code>value</code></td><td>The current value.</td></tr><tr><td><code>min</code>, <code>max</code></td><td>The range.</td></tr><tr><td><code>low</code>, <code>high</code></td><td>Where the low and the high zones start.</td></tr><tr><td><code>optimum</code></td><td>The best value, which decides what counts as good.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="el-skeleton"><code>skeleton</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>اسکلت</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A grey placeholder that pulses while the real content loads. Set its size with <code>width</code> and <code>height</code>, and shape it with <code>radius</code>, here a circle for an avatar.</p>
{{example.ds-display-skeleton}}
<h3 id="el-spinner"><code>spinner</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>چرخنده</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A small rotating icon for work in progress. It has <code>role="status"</code>, takes the colour of the text around it, and fits inside a <code>button</code>. Its <code>content</code> is the label for screen readers and defaults to "Loading".</p>
{{example.ds-display-spinner}}
<h2 id="media">Avatars and ratios</h2>
<h3 id="el-avatar"><code>avatar</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>آواتار</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A round picture of a person. Put an <code>avatar image</code> and an <code>avatar fallback</code> inside. The fallback, usually initials, shows under the image and stays visible when the image is missing or fails to load, as in the fourth avatar. <code>size</code> is <code>sm</code>, <code>default</code> or <code>lg</code>, which are 24, 32 and 40 pixels. An <code>avatar badge</code> adds a status dot, <code>avatar group</code> overlaps several avatars, and <code>avatar group count</code> is the bubble that says how many more there are.</p>
{{example.ds-display-avatar}}
<h3 id="el-aspect-ratio"><code>aspect ratio</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>نسبت تصویر</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A box that keeps a fixed shape whatever its width. <code>ratio</code> is written as a string such as <code>"16/9"</code> or <code>"1/1"</code>. Images and videos inside it are cropped to fill it. The ratio is set inline, so it works with any design.</p>
{{example.ds-display-aspect-ratio}}
<h2 id="data">Tables and text</h2>
<h3 id="el-table"><code>table</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>جدول</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The ordinary <code>table</code> with its <code>header</code>, <code>main</code>, <code>footer</code>, <code>row</code>, <code>column</code> and <code>caption</code> elements, now styled: light dividers, a hover tint on rows and a muted footer. A row with <code>selected = true</code> gets the muted background. The table is wrapped in a container that scrolls sideways when it is too wide for the screen, so nothing about how you write it changes.</p>
{{example.ds-display-table}}
<h3 id="el-typography"><code>typography</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>نگارش</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A container that gives the text inside the shadcn look: <code>heading</code> levels 1 to 4, <code>paragraph</code>, <code>block quote</code>, <code>list</code> and <code>code</code>. Four classes change a paragraph: <code>lead</code> for an introduction, <code>large</code>, <code>small</code> and <code>muted</code>. Pages that do not use <code>typography</code> keep the plain browser defaults.</p>
{{example.ds-display-typography}}
<h2 id="empty">Empty states and entries</h2>
<h3 id="el-empty"><code>empty</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>خالی</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The screen to show when there is nothing to list yet. The parts are <code>empty header</code>, <code>empty media</code>, <code>empty title</code>, <code>empty description</code> and <code>empty content</code> for the call to action. <code>empty media</code> takes <code>variant = "icon"</code> for a small muted square that holds an icon or a letter.</p>
{{example.ds-display-empty}}
<h3 id="el-entry"><code>entry</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>مدخل</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A row with a picture, a title, a description and actions, for lists of people, files or settings. It is shadcn's Item. In Salam <code>item</code> already means a list item, so this one is called <code>entry</code>. Wrap several in an <code>entry group</code>. With <code>url</code> the whole entry is a link with a hover tint. The description is cut after two lines. Inside, <code>entry media</code> holds the picture, <code>entry content</code> the <code>entry title</code> and <code>entry description</code>, and <code>entry header</code> and <code>entry footer</code> add a row above or below.</p>
{{example.ds-display-entry}}

<h2 id="navigation">Navigation and scrolling</h2>
<h3 id="el-breadcrumb"><code>breadcrumb</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>مسیر</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The trail that shows where the visitor is. Build it from <code>breadcrumb item</code> with a <code>link</code> inside, <code>breadcrumb separator</code>, <code>breadcrumb ellipsis</code> for hidden levels and, last, <code>breadcrumb page</code> for the current page, which is marked with <code>aria-current</code>. The separator is a chevron, mirrored in right to left pages, or the content you give it, like the slash above.</p>
{{example.ds-display-breadcrumb}}
<h3 id="el-pagination"><code>pagination</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>صفحه بندی</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Links to the pages of a long list. Use <code>pagination link</code> for a number, with <code>active = true</code> for the current one, <code>pagination previous</code> and <code>pagination next</code> at the ends, and <code>pagination ellipsis</code> for skipped numbers. The default labels are "Previous" and "Next", and <code>content</code> replaces them. The arrows turn around in right to left pages.</p>
{{example.ds-display-pagination}}
<h3 id="el-scroll-area"><code>scroll area</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>ناحیه پیمایش</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A box that scrolls inside the page, with thin rounded scrollbars. Give it a <code>height</code> or <code>width</code>, otherwise it grows with its content and has nothing to scroll.</p>
{{example.ds-display-scroll-area}}
