<h2 id="panels">Panels that stay on the page</h2>
<p>Overlays are drawn above the page or hide part of it until asked. Behaviour (keyboard, focus, ARIA, events) does not depend on the design: with <code>design = with <code>design = &quot;none&quot;</code> they still workquot;nonewith <code>design = &quot;none&quot;</code> they still workquot;</code> they still work (only the look is dropped). <h3 id="el-tabs"><code>tabs</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>زبانه ها</code>)</span></h3>
<p>Tabs show one panel at a time. The <code>tabs</code> root holds a <code>tab list</code> of <code>tab trigger</code> buttons and one <code>tab content</code> per trigger; the <code>value</code> of a trigger and its content must match, and <code>default value</code> picks the open one. The arrow keys (aware of right to left text), Home and End move and select. The example shows the default list, the <code>line</code> list variant and vertical tabs.</p>

<h3 id="el-accordion"><code>accordion</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>آکاردئون</code>)</span></h3>
<p>An accordion is a stack of sections that open and close. Each <code>accordion item</code> holds an <code>accordion trigger</code> and an <code>accordion content</code>. With <code>type = &quot;single&quot;</code> opening one closes the others; <code>collapsible = true</code> lets the open one be closed again; <code>type = &quot;multiple&quot;</code> keeps several open. Put <code>open = true</code> on an item to start open. Opening and closing animate for 200 ms unless the visitor asked for reduced motion.</p>

<h2 id="dialogs">Dialogs, sheets and drawers</h2>
<h3 id="el-dialog"><code>dialog</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پنجره گفتگو</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A dialog is a modal window over the page. It is a native <code>dialog</code> opened with <code>showModal</code>, so focus stays inside, the page behind is inert, Escape closes it and focus returns to the button. Give it an <code>id</code> and set <code>opens</code> to that id on a <code>button</code> or a <code>dialog trigger</code>. Close it with <code>closes = true</code> on a button, with a <code>dialog close</code> element, or with the corner X (turn that off with <code>show close = false</code>). A click outside the window also closes it; write <code>close outside = false</code> to turn that off so that only the buttons and Escape close it. <code>width</code> sets the size. Press the buttons in the example to open them. Inside, <code>dialog header</code> holds the <code>dialog title</code> and <code>dialog description</code>, and <code>dialog footer</code> holds the buttons; the example uses all four.</p>

<h3 id="el-alert-dialog"><code>alert dialog</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پنجره هشدار</code>)</span></h3>
<p>An alert dialog asks for a decision before something that cannot be undone. It has no corner X, and the visitor normally picks <code>alert dialog cancel</code> or <code>alert dialog action</code>. <code>size = &quot;sm&quot;</code> makes the small form. Open it with the buttons below. The question goes in <code>alert dialog header</code> as an <code>alert dialog title</code> and an <code>alert dialog description</code>, and the two choices go in <code>alert dialog footer</code>. It also closes on an outside click, like the other windows; write <code>close outside = false</code> when the visitor has to choose a button.</p>

<h3 id="el-sheet"><code>sheet</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>برگه کناری</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A sheet is a dialog that slides in from an edge of the screen, for settings or a menu. <code>side</code> picks the edge: <code>top</code>, <code>right</code> (the default), <code>bottom</code> or <code>left</code>. It opens and closes like a dialog. The example has one button for each side; the left one has no corner X. Each sheet in the example is built from a <code>sheet header</code> with a <code>sheet title</code> and <code>sheet description</code>, and a <code>sheet footer</code> for the actions. A click outside closes it unless you write <code>close outside = false</code>.</p>
{{example.ds-overlay-sheet}}
<p>For a page that is read in both directions use <code>start</code> and <code>end</code> instead of <code>left</code> and <code>right</code>. <code>start</code> is the left edge in a left to right page and the right edge in a right to left page; <code>end</code> is the opposite one. Both the sheet and the drawer take them, and the slide animation and the drawer's drag to dismiss follow the direction too.</p>

<h3 id="el-drawer"><code>drawer</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>کشو</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A drawer is a sheet made for touch: by default it rises from the bottom with a handle, and dragging it down dismisses it. <code>side</code> moves it to another edge. Its content uses the same <code>drawer header</code>, <code>drawer title</code>, <code>drawer description</code> and <code>drawer footer</code> as a sheet. A click outside closes it unless you write <code>close outside = false</code>.</p>

<h2 id="floating">Popovers and tooltips</h2>
<h3 id="el-popover"><code>popover</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پنجره شناور</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A popover is a small panel next to its trigger, for a bit of extra content or a mini form. The root holds a <code>popover trigger</code> and a <code>popover content</code>. A trigger with no children draws an outline button from its <code>content</code>; put your own <code>button</code> inside to use another. On the content, <code>side</code>, <code>align</code> and <code>side offset</code> place it; it flips when there is no room. Escape or a click outside closes it. Press the buttons to open them. Inside the content, <code>popover header</code> groups a <code>popover title</code> and a <code>popover description</code>.</p>

<h3 id="el-tooltip"><code>tooltip</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>راهنما</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A tooltip is a short hint that shows on hover and on keyboard focus, with a small arrow. <code>side</code> on the <code>tooltip content</code> picks the edge, and <code>delay</code> on the root waits that many milliseconds first (default 0). Hover the buttons in the example.</p>

<h2 id="menus">Menus</h2>
<h3 id="el-dropdown-menu"><code>dropdown menu</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>منوی کشویی</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A dropdown menu opens a list of actions from a button. Inside <code>menu content</code> you can put <code>menu item</code> (with <code>variant = &quot;destructive&quot;</code>, <code>disabled</code> and a <code>menu shortcut</code>), <code>menu checkbox item</code>, a <code>menu radio group</code> of <code>menu radio item</code>, <code>menu label</code>, <code>menu group</code>, <code>menu separator</code> and a submenu made of <code>menu sub</code>, <code>menu sub trigger</code> and <code>menu sub content</code>. The keyboard works as in a desktop menu: arrows, Home, End, typing the first letters, Enter or Space to choose, Escape to close. Each choice fires <code>ds-select</code> with <code>detail.value</code> and <code>detail.checked</code>. Press the button to open it.</p>

<h3 id="el-context-menu"><code>context menu</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>منوی راست کلیک</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A context menu opens at the pointer on a right click (or long press) over its trigger area. The <code>context menu trigger</code> wraps whatever should react, and <code>context menu content</code> holds the same items as a dropdown menu. Right click the dashed box.</p>

<h3 id="el-menubar"><code>menubar</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>نوار منو</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A menubar is a row of menus like File, Edit and View in a desktop app. Each <code>menubar menu</code> holds a <code>menubar trigger</code> and a <code>menu content</code>. Once one is open, hovering another trigger switches to it, and Left and Right move between them. Click File in the example.</p>

<h3 id="el-navigation-menu"><code>navigation menu</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>منوی ناوبری</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A navigation menu is the main menu of a site. <code>navigation menu list</code> holds <code>navigation menu item</code>s; an item has either a <code>navigation menu trigger</code> with a <code>navigation menu content</code> full of links, or a plain <code>navigation menu link</code> with <code>url</code> and <code>active</code> for the current page. It opens on hover or click and closes on Escape or a click outside. Hover Learn in the example.</p>
