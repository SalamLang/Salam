<h2 id="feedback">Toast</h2>
<h3 id="el-toast"><code>toaster</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>اعلان ها</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A toast is a small message that appears in a corner, stays a few seconds and goes away by itself. Put one <code>toaster</code> on the page: it holds the toasts and brings their styles and script. Then give any <code>button</code> a <code>toast</code> attribute, and pressing it shows that message. <code>toast description</code> adds a second line and <code>toast variant</code> picks the colour and icon.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-toast}}
<p>A toast stops its timer while the pointer or the focus is on it, and the reader can swipe it sideways or use the close button to dismiss it.</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Where</th><th>Attribute</th><th>Values</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td><code>toaster</code></td><td><code>placement</code><br><span class="ref-alt">جانمایی</span></td><td><code>top left</code>, <code>top center</code>, <code>top right</code>, <code>bottom left</code>, <code>bottom center</code>, <code>bottom right</code> (default).</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>toaster</code></td><td><code>duration</code>, <code>limit</code>, <code>close button</code></td><td>Milliseconds before it goes (4000), how many show at once (3), and whether each toast has a close button.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>button</code></td><td><code>toast</code>, <code>toast description</code></td><td>The message and the second line.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>button</code></td><td><code>toast variant</code></td><td><code>default</code>, <code>success</code>, <code>error</code>, <code>warning</code>, <code>info</code>.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>From your own script the same thing is a function: <code>salamToast(title, {description, variant, duration, action, closeButton, id})</code>, with the shortcuts <code>salamToast.success</code>, <code>.error</code>, <code>.warning</code> and <code>.info</code>, and <code>salamToast.dismiss(id)</code> to close one early.</p>

<h2 id="pick">Command, combobox and select</h2>
<h3 id="el-command"><code>command</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>فرمان</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A command palette is a search box with a list under it that filters as the reader types. Build it from <code>command input</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>ورودی فرمان</code>)</span>, <code>command list</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>فهرست فرمان</code>)</span>, <code>command group</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>گروه فرمان</code>)</span>, with a <code>heading</code>, <code>command item</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>گزینه فرمان</code>)</span>, <code>command shortcut</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>میانبر فرمان</code>)</span>, <code>command separator</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>جداکننده فرمان</code>)</span> and <code>command empty</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>خالی فرمان</code>)</span>, which shows when nothing matches. An item has a <code>value</code>, extra search words in <code>keywords</code>, and can be <code>disabled</code>. The arrow keys, Home and End move, Enter or a click activates, and the item fires a <code>ds-select</code> event whose <code>detail.value</code> is its value.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-command}}
<p>Type "date" and the Calendar item stays: its <code>keywords</code> match even though the visible text does not.</p>
<h3 id="el-command-dialog"><code>command dialog</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پنجره فرمان</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The usual place for a palette is a modal. <code>command dialog</code> wraps a <code>command</code> in one. Open it from any button with <code>opens</code> and the dialog's <code>id</code>, or let the reader press a key: <code>hotkey = "k"</code> opens it with Ctrl or Cmd plus K.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-command-dialog}}

<h3 id="el-combobox"><code>combobox</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>جعبه ترکیبی</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A combobox is a field that opens a searchable list. Its items are <code>combobox item</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>گزینه جعبه ترکیبی</code>)</span> elements. The chosen item shows a check mark, and choosing it again clears the value. With a <code>name</code> the value travels in a hidden input, so a form sends it. The second box below is <code>clearable</code> and starts with a <code>value</code>.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-combobox}}

<tr><td><code>name</code>, <code>value</code></td><td>The form field name and the starting value.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>placeholder</code>, <code>search placeholder</code>, <code>empty text</code></td><td>Text of the closed box, of the search field and of the "nothing found" line.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>clearable</code>, <code>disabled</code></td><td>Show a clear button; turn the control off.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>ds-change</code></td><td>The event fired when the value changes.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

<h3 id="el-select-box"><code>select box</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>جعبه انتخاب</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A select box is a custom list box that replaces the browser's <code>select</code> where you want the look to match. It is made of a <code>select trigger</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>دکمه جعبه انتخاب</code>)</span> whose content is the placeholder, and a <code>select content</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>محتوای جعبه انتخاب</code>)</span> with <code>select item</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>گزینه جعبه انتخاب</code>)</span>, <code>select group</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>گروه جعبه انتخاب</code>)</span> and <code>select separator</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>جداکننده جعبه انتخاب</code>)</span>. The trigger's <code>size</code> is <code>default</code> or <code>sm</code>. The keyboard works: arrows, Home and End, Enter or Space, Escape, and typing the start of a word.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-select}}
<p>Like the combobox, it keeps its value in a hidden input when it has a <code>name</code>, and fires <code>ds-change</code>.</p>

<h2 id="dates">Calendar and date picker</h2>
<h3 id="el-calendar"><code>calendar</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>تقویم</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A month grid with previous and next buttons, today marked, and full keyboard support: arrows move by a day or a week, Home and End go to the ends of the week, PageUp and PageDown change the month, and Shift with them changes the year. Values are always written as ISO Gregorian dates, <code>2026-11-18</code>, whichever calendar is shown. A range is <code>from/to</code>, such as <code>2026-11-10/2026-11-16</code>.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-calendar-modes}}
<p>The first calendar picks one day, the second a range, and the third shows the limits: <code>min</code> and <code>max</code> grey out days outside, <code>week start</code> chooses the first column, <code>disabled days</code> lists weekday numbers to block (0 is Sunday, so <code>"0,6"</code> blocks the weekend) and <code>disabled dates</code> lists single days.</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>Values</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td><code>calendar</code><br><span class="ref-alt">تقویم</span></td><td><code>gregorian</code> (default), <code>jalali</code>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>mode</code><br><span class="ref-alt">شیوه انتخاب</span></td><td><code>single</code> (default), <code>range</code>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>value</code>, <code>min</code>, <code>max</code></td><td>ISO dates; for a range, <code>from/to</code>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>week start</code></td><td>0 to 6, Sunday is 0. The default is 6 (Saturday) for Jalali.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>disabled days</code>, <code>disabled dates</code></td><td>Weekday numbers such as <code>"0,6"</code>; an ISO list.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>ds-change</code></td><td>Event with <code>detail.value</code>, or <code>detail.from</code> and <code>detail.to</code> for a range.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<h3 id="el-calendar-jalali">The Jalali calendar</h3>
<p>Write <code>calendar = "jalali"</code> and the grid becomes the Persian calendar: months such as Aban, a year like 1405, a week that starts on Saturday and a right to left layout. On an English page the month and weekday names and the digits are English, as in the previews here, and on a Persian page the same calendar shows Persian names and digits. Only what the reader sees changes: the value you read and write stays an ISO Gregorian date, so the rest of your code never converts anything.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-calendar-jalali}}
<h3 id="el-date-picker"><code>date picker</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>انتخابگر تاریخ</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A date picker is a button that opens a calendar in a popover. It takes every attribute of the calendar, plus <code>name</code>, <code>placeholder</code> and <code>disabled</code>, and a hidden input holds the ISO value for a form. The three below are a single date, a range and a Jalali date with a starting value.</p>

<h2 id="layout">Carousel, resizable and sidebar</h2>
<h3 id="el-carousel"><code>carousel</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>چرخ‌فلک</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A carousel shows one slide at a time and scrolls between them with snapping. It is made of a <code>carousel content</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>محتوای چرخ‌فلک</code>)</span> that holds the slides, each a <code>carousel item</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>اسلاید</code>)</span>, and optional <code>carousel previous</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>قبلی چرخ‌فلک</code>)</span> and <code>carousel next</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>بعدی چرخ‌فلک</code>)</span> buttons, which sit outside the slides and are disabled at the ends unless <code>loop</code> is on. The arrow keys, Home and End, and dragging with the mouse all work. <code>dots</code> adds the row of position dots.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-carousel}}
<p>A vertical carousel needs a fixed height on its content. This one also has <code>autoplay</code>, in milliseconds, which pauses while the pointer or focus is on it.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-carousel-vertical}}

<tr><td><code>orientation</code><br><span class="ref-alt">راستا</span></td><td><code>horizontal</code> (default), <code>vertical</code>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>loop</code>, <code>dots</code></td><td>Wrap around at the ends; show the dots.</td></tr>

<h3 id="el-resizable"><code>resizable</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>قابل تغییر</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A resizable group lays out panels side by side (or one above the other) with draggable handles between them. The panels are <code>resizable panel</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>بخش قابل تغییر</code>)</span>, the handles <code>resizable handle</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>دستگیره قابل تغییر</code>)</span>. A panel's <code>size</code> is a percentage; panels without one share what is left, and <code>min size</code> and <code>max size</code> keep it in bounds. A handle moves with the mouse or with the arrow keys (5 percent a step), Home and End. <code>grip = true</code> draws the little grip. Groups nest, as the vertical one inside the right panel does here. Every change fires <code>ds-resize</code> with <code>detail.sizes</code>.</p>
{{example.ds-extras-resizable}}

<h3 id="el-sidebar"><code>sidebar</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>پنل نوار کناری</code>)</span></h3>
<p>The word <code>sidebar</code> is already another name for <code>aside</code>, so the shadcn side panel is called <code>sidebar panel</code>. It lives in a <code>sidebar provider</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>فراهم کننده نوار کناری</code>)</span> with the main area in <code>sidebar inset</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>محتوای اصلی نوار کناری</code>)</span>. Inside the panel you use <code>sidebar header</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>سرایند نوار کناری</code>)</span>, <code>sidebar content</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>محتوای نوار کناری</code>)</span>, <code>sidebar footer</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>پاورقی نوار کناری</code>)</span>, <code>sidebar group</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>گروه نوار کناری</code>)</span> with <code>sidebar group label</code> and <code>sidebar group content</code>, <code>sidebar menu</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>منوی نوار کناری</code>)</span>, <code>sidebar menu item</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>آیتم منوی نوار کناری</code>)</span>, <code>sidebar menu button</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>دکمه منوی نوار کناری</code>)</span>, <code>sidebar menu badge</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>نشان منوی نوار کناری</code>)</span> and <code>sidebar separator</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>جداکننده نوار کناری</code>)</span>. <code>sidebar trigger</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>دکمه نوار کناری</code>)</span> is the toggle button, and Ctrl or Cmd plus B does the same.</p>

<p>The preview needs a window wider than 768 pixels to show the panel. Below that width the panel becomes a sheet that slides over the page, opened by the trigger.</p>

<h2 id="charts">Charts</h2>
<h3 id="el-chart"><code>chart</code><span class="ref-alt"> (Persian: <code>نمودار</code>)</span></h3>
<p>A chart is drawn in the browser as SVG, redraws when the window resizes and shows a tooltip on hover. The data lives in the page: a <code>chart series</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>سری نمودار</code>)</span> is one data set with a <code>label</code> and an optional <code>tint</code> colour, and each <code>chart point</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>نقطه نمودار</code>)</span> has a <code>label</code> and a <code>value</code>. Points are matched to categories by position, and the first series names the categories. Without a <code>tint</code> the series take the theme colours <code>var(--chart-1)</code> to <code>var(--chart-5)</code> in order. The attribute <code>type</code> <span class="ref-alt">(<code>نوع</code>)</span> picks the kind.</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table class="ref-table"><thead><tr><th>Attribute</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td><code>type</code></td><td><code>bar</code>, <code>line</code>, <code>area</code>, <code>pie</code>, <code>donut</code>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>chart height</code></td><td>Height in pixels, 250 by default.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>legend</code>, <code>label</code></td><td>Show the legend; give the chart an accessible name.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>grid</code></td><td><code>"false"</code> hides the grid lines.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>stacked</code></td><td>Stack the series, for bar and area.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>center</code></td><td>The caption under the total in the middle of a donut.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
