pkg.intro <<
<p>With a remote procedure call one program calls another program's methods as if they were its own routines. On the server side you tie each method to a routine with <code>Register</code>; that routine takes the call's number, reads the parameters with <code>CallParamInt</code> and the like, and answers with <code>Reply...</code> or <code>Fail</code>.</p>
<p>The messages are exchanged with the JSON codec (JSON-RPC 2.0) or MessagePack, and over TCP they are separated by one of three framings, line, header (like LSP) or length prefixed; the client and the server must have the same framing. <code>HandleText</code> does the same thing without a network, so you can put RPC behind an <a href="../net-http/">net/http</a> route.</p>
>>
