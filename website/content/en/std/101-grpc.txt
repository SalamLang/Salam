pkg.intro <<
<p>gRPC is a way of calling a remote procedure that runs over <a href="../net-http2/">HTTP/2</a>, and it is common in microservices. On the server side you register each method under its path, like <code>/demo.Greeter/SayHello</code>; the method routine reads the client's message with <code>Recv</code>, answers with <code>Send</code> and ends it with <code>SetStatus</code> or <code>Fail</code>.</p>
<p>On the client side, <code>UnaryCall</code> is a plain request, and for streams you use <code>StartCall</code>, <code>SendMessage</code>, <code>RecvMessage</code> and <code>FinishCall</code>. Messages are bytes; you usually build them with <a href="../encoding-protobuf/">protobuf</a>.</p>
>>
