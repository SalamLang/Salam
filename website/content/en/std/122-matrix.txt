pkg.intro <<
<p>This package is complete linear algebra over 64 bit floats: from making a matrix and multiplying through to solving systems, decompositions, eigenvalues and singular values, and statistical tools such as principal component analysis. To solve one system, call <code>Solve</code>; for several systems with one matrix, <code>Decompose</code> once and <code>LUSolve</code> many times.</p>
<p>Matrices hold floats and the results carry a little rounding error; compare with <code>AllClose</code> and display with <code>Chop</code>. For neural networks see <a href="../tensor/">tensor</a>.</p>
>>
