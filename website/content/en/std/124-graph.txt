pkg.intro <<
<p>This package has more than two hundred graph algorithms. Make a graph with <code>NewUndirected</code> or <code>NewDirected</code>, add edges with <code>add_edge</code> and <code>add_weighted</code>, then call <code>build</code>; or read the whole thing from text in one go with <code>FromEdgeList</code>. Nodes are numbered from zero to n minus one.</p>
<p>Everything from traversal and components to shortest paths, spanning trees, flow and cuts, matching, coloring, communities and centrality is ready to use. You can export the result with <code>ToDot</code> or <code>ToMermaid</code> to draw it. The algorithms that solve hard problems, such as maximum clique and Hamiltonian cycle, are meant for small graphs.</p>
>>
