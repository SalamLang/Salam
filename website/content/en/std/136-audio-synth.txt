pkg.intro <<
<p>Everything else in this family reads sound that already exists. This package makes it. Six waveforms, an envelope to shape a note, three colours of noise, and four ways of building a voice that sounds like something rather than like a test tone.</p>
<p>The quickest way in is <code>Tone</code> for a bare waveform or <code>ToneEnvelope</code> for a note that starts and stops the way an instrument does. To play a phrase, fill a vector of <code>Note</code> and hand it to <code>RenderNotes</code>, which mixes and pans them into one clip.</p>
<h3>Things to know</h3>
<ul>
<li>An oscillator carries its own phase, so stepping it with <code>OscNext</code> in a loop gives a continuous tone. <code>WaveValue</code> is the stateless form.</li>
<li>A note lasts longer than you asked: <code>ADSRTotal</code> adds the release onto the hold, and that is the length <code>ToneEnvelope</code> gives you.</li>
<li><code>Chord</code> divides by the number of notes, so three notes together do not clip. <code>RenderNotes</code> does not, so give its notes a gain that leaves room.</li>
<li>For which notes to play, see <a href="/std/audio/music/">audio/music</a>, which also carries the Persian dastgah; to play a file of notes, see <a href="/std/audio/midi/">audio/midi</a>.</li>
</ul>
>>

doc.WaveNoise = White noise, no pitch at all.
doc.WavePulse = A square whose high and low parts are uneven.
doc.WaveSaw = A sawtooth, bright and full of every harmonic.
doc.WaveSine = A pure tone, one frequency and nothing else.
doc.WaveSquare = A square wave, hollow and full of odd harmonics.
doc.WaveTriangle = A triangle, softer than a square.
doc.ADSRAt = The envelope level at a time, given how long the note is held.
doc.ADSRTotal = How long a note really lasts, including the release after the hold.
doc.Additive = A tone built by adding harmonics, each quieter than the one below.
doc.Chord = A clip of several frequencies together, scaled so they do not clip.
doc.FMTone = Frequency modulation: one oscillator bending another, which gets you bells and basses cheaply.
doc.KarplusStrong = A plucked string, made by filtering a burst of noise around a delay line.
doc.NewOsc = An oscillator ready to step.
doc.NoiseBrown = Brown noise, falling off faster still.
doc.NoisePink = Pink noise, falling off with frequency.
doc.NoiseWhite = White noise, flat across the spectrum.
doc.NoteAt = Shorthand for one Note in a phrase.
doc.OscNext = The next sample, moving the oscillator on by one.
doc.RenderNotes = Renders a whole phrase into one clip, mixing the notes and panning them.
doc.Tone = A clip of one waveform at one pitch.
doc.ToneEnvelope = A clip of one note with an envelope and a gain, which is how a note should sound rather than a test tone.
doc.WaveValue = One point of a waveform at a phase, without an oscillator.
doc.ADSR = An envelope: how a note rises, settles, holds and falls.
doc.ADSR.attack = Seconds from silence to full.
doc.ADSR.decay = Seconds from full down to the sustain level.
doc.ADSR.sustain = The level a held note rests at.
doc.ADSR.release = Seconds from the sustain level back to silence.
doc.Note = One note in a phrase: which, when, how long and how loud.
doc.Note.note = The MIDI note number.
doc.Note.start = When it starts, in seconds.
doc.Note.length = How long it is held, in seconds.
doc.Note.gain = How loud it is.
doc.Note.wave = Which waveform plays it.
doc.Note.pan = Where it sits between the speakers, 0 left to 1 right.
doc.Osc = An oscillator: a waveform, a pitch, and where in the cycle it is.
doc.Osc.wave = Which waveform it makes.
doc.Osc.freq = Its pitch in hertz.
doc.Osc.phase = Where in the cycle it is, from 0 to 1.
doc.Osc.duty = How much of a pulse cycle is high.
doc.Osc.rate = The sample rate it is stepping at.
