pkg.intro <<
<p>NATS is a small, fast message bus. <code>net/nats</code> is a client for it written in Salam, with no native library. <code>Connect</code> opens a <code>Conn</code> from a URL such as <code>nats://127.0.0.1:4222</code>, and <code>ConnectWith</code> adds an <code>Options</code> value (start from <code>DefaultOptions</code>), you publish to a subject, subscribe to one, or send a request and wait for the reply. Subjects are dotted names such as <code>orders.created</code>, and <code>ValidSubject</code> tells you whether a name is acceptable before you use it.</p>

<h2 id="guide-headers">Headers</h2>
<p>A <code>Header</code> carries key and value pairs next to the payload. <code>NewHeader</code> makes one, <code>HeaderAdd</code> appends a value, <code>HeaderSet</code> replaces the values of a key, <code>HeaderGet</code> and <code>HeaderValues</code> read them, and <code>HeaderEncode</code> and <code>HeaderParse</code> turn a header to and from its wire form.</p>
{{example.net-nats-headers}}

<h2 id="guide-jetstream">JetStream</h2>
<p>JetStream keeps messages. <code>AddStream</code> creates a stream over some subjects, <code>JSPublish</code> publishes and returns a <code>PubAck</code> once the stream has stored the message, and <code>AddConsumer</code> with <code>Fetch</code> reads messages back in batches. After handling a message call <code>Ack</code>; <code>Nak</code> asks for a redelivery, <code>InProgress</code> extends the wait and <code>Term</code> gives up on the message. <code>StreamInfo</code>, <code>PurgeStream</code> and <code>DeleteStream</code> inspect and clean up.</p>
>>
