pkg.intro <<
<p>A slug is the readable part of an address, like <code>my-first-post</code> in <code>/blog/my-first-post</code>. This package turns a title in any language into one. It is written in Salam, uses fixed tables only (no models, no network, no files), keeps no state, and is safe to call from many threads at once.</p>
<p><code>Make</code> is the whole story for most programs: it transliterates to ASCII, lowercases, drops emoji, spells out <code>&amp;</code> and <code>%</code>, and joins the words with a hyphen. <code>MakeWith</code> takes an <code>Options</code> value when you need another separator, a length limit, stop words, your own replacements or native letters.</p>
<h2 id="guide-basics">Titles to slugs in one call</h2>
<p>Punctuation and spaces become one separator, apostrophes vanish without leaving a gap (<code>what's</code> is <code>whats</code>), accents are removed, symbols such as <code>&amp;</code>, <code>@</code>, <code>%</code>, <code>+</code> and currency signs are written as words, and emoji are dropped.</p>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-basics}}
<h2 id="guide-languages">Many languages, one table per script</h2>
<p>The script of the text picks the table. <code>DetectScript</code> names the dominant script of a text: <code>latin</code>, <code>cyrillic</code>, <code>greek</code>, <code>arabic</code>, <code>hebrew</code>, <code>devanagari</code>, <code>thai</code>, <code>japanese</code>, <code>korean</code> or <code>han</code>, and an empty text for digits and symbols only. The tables follow the usual conventions of each language, so the result is the spelling readers expect, not a letter-by-letter code.</p>

With the language <code>de</code>, <code>ä ö ü</code> become <code>ae oe ue</code>; with <code>da</code> or <code>no</code>, <code>å ø</code> become <code>aa oe</code>.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Cyrillic</td><td>Russian by default, Ukrainian (<code>г</code> is <code>h</code>, <code>и</code> is <code>y</code>, <code>ї</code> is <code>yi</code> at the start of a word and <code>i</code> inside it), Bulgarian (<code>ъ</code> is <code>a</code>, <code>щ</code> is <code>sht</code>), Serbian and Macedonian without diacritics, Belarusian.</td></tr>

<h2 id="guide-persian">Persian and Arabic</h2>
<p>Persian and Arabic share a script, so the language is told apart by the letters: any of <code>پ چ ژ گ ک ی</code> or the digits <code>۰</code> to <code>۹</code> mean Persian; <code>ة ى ي ك أ إ</code> without them mean Arabic; text with neither is read as Persian. Pass <code>lang = "fa"</code> or <code>"ar"</code> to decide yourself.</p>
<ul>
<li>Persian digits and Arabic-Indic digits become <code>0</code> to <code>9</code> everywhere.</li>
<li>Tashkeel (harakat), shadda, tatweel and the Quranic marks are removed; presentation forms (<code>ﻻ</code>, <code>ﺳ</code>) are first turned into the plain letters.</li>
<li>The zero-width non-joiner is a word boundary: <code>می‌خواهم</code> becomes <code>mi-khvahm</code>.</li>
<li>Arabic <code>ي</code> and <code>ك</code> are the same letters as Persian <code>ی</code> and <code>ک</code>.</li>
<li>Letters follow the common conventions: <code>خ</code> is <code>kh</code>, <code>ش</code> is <code>sh</code>, <code>چ</code> is <code>ch</code>, <code>ژ</code> is <code>zh</code>, <code>گ</code> is <code>g</code>, <code>ع</code> is <code>a</code>; Persian <code>ق</code> is <code>gh</code> and <code>ث ذ ض</code> are <code>s z z</code>, Arabic <code>ق</code> is <code>q</code> and <code>ث ذ ض</code> are <code>th dh d</code>.</li>
<li><code>و</code> and <code>ی</code> are guessed from their neighbours. In Persian, <code>و</code> is <code>v</code> at the start of a word or before a vowel letter, <code>u</code> otherwise, and <code>va</code> when it stands alone as the word "and"; <code>ی</code> is <code>y</code> at the start, next to a vowel letter, and <code>i</code> otherwise; <code>ای</code> at the start of a word is <code>i</code> (<code>ایران</code> is <code>iran</code>). In Arabic, <code>و</code> is <code>w</code>.</li>
<li>Short vowels are not written in Persian or Arabic text and are not guessed, so <code>سلام</code> is <code>slam</code> and <code>محمد</code> is <code>mhmd</code>. When the pretty English spelling matters (a person or a place), set it with <code>AddReplacement</code>.</li>
</ul>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-persian}}
<h2 id="guide-options">Options</h2>
<p>Start from <code>DefaultOptions</code> (or a preset) and change what you need. Stop words are compared with the finished words, so write them in any script and case: they go through the same transliteration as the text. If every word of a title is a stop word, the words are kept so the slug is not empty. Replacements are plain text, applied left to right before anything else, so <code>C++</code> can become <code>cpp</code> before the plus signs turn into words. <code>fallback</code> is returned when nothing is left.</p>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-options}}
<h2 id="guide-length">Limiting the length</h2>
<p><code>max_length</code> counts characters, not bytes. The slug is cut between words, never in the middle of one: whole words are kept while they fit, with their separators. Only when the very first word is longer than the limit is it cut, so the result is never empty for a non-empty title. The <code>URL</code> preset limits slugs to 100 characters.</p>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-length}}
<h2 id="guide-unicode">Keeping the native letters</h2>
<p>With <code>keep_unicode</code> the letters of every script stay as they are: only the case is folded (Latin, Greek and Cyrillic), Persian forms are unified (<code>ي</code> to <code>ی</code>, <code>ك</code> to <code>ک</code>), digits become ASCII, marks that do not belong in a word are removed and separators work as usual. Modern browsers and search engines handle such addresses, and they read better for Persian, Russian or Greek sites. Input should be in normal form NFC. Without <code>keep_unicode</code>, Han characters are still kept because no table exists; set <code>keep_han</code> to false to drop them.</p>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-unicode}}
<h2 id="guide-unique">Unique slugs</h2>
<p><code>Unique</code> takes a function that says whether a slug is already used (a database lookup, a set, a directory listing) and returns the slug itself or the first free one of <code>slug-2</code>, <code>slug-3</code> and so on. <code>UniqueWith</code> picks the separator and <code>Numbered</code> builds one numbered name. After ten thousand tries the number reached so far is returned, so a function that always answers yes cannot hang the program.</p>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-unique}}
<h2 id="guide-validate">Checking a slug</h2>
<p><code>IsSlug</code> accepts only what <code>Make</code> can produce with the default options: lowercase letters and digits joined by single hyphens, none at either end. <code>IsSlugWith</code> checks against the options you used, including the separator, the length limit and native letters.</p>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-validate}}
<h2 id="guide-presets">Presets</h2>
<p><code>URL</code> is the default with a 100 character limit. <code>Filename</code> keeps case, uses <code>_</code>, allows dots and hyphens, limits to 200 characters, never starts or ends with a dot and falls back to <code>file</code>, so <code>../etc/passwd</code> cannot escape a directory. <code>Snake</code> uses <code>_</code>. <code>Identifier</code> uses <code>_</code>, never starts with a digit and falls back to <code>_</code>, which is what you want for a variable or a column name. <code>Unicode</code> keeps native letters.</p>
{{example.encoding-slug-guide-presets}}
<p>What people get wrong:</p>
<ul>
<li>Expecting <code>slam</code> to be written <code>salam</code>. Persian and Arabic text has no short vowels, and the package does not invent them; use <code>AddReplacement</code> for names.</li>
<li>Using the slug as a key without <code>Unique</code>. Two different titles can give the same slug, and an empty title gives an empty one unless <code>fallback</code> is set.</li>
<li>Counting bytes. <code>max_length</code> counts characters; with <code>keep_unicode</code> a Persian slug of 50 characters is about 100 bytes.</li>
<li>Splitting <code>camelCase</code>: <code>fooBar</code> is <code>foobar</code>. Put a space in the title if you want two words.</li>
<li>Freeing an <code>Options</code> that holds stop words or replacements twice. Copies of an <code>Options</code> share those two lists, so call <code>FreeOptions</code> once for the family.</li>
</ul>
<p>Every string returned is new and belongs to the caller. For percent-encoding see <a href="/std/encoding/url/">encoding.url</a>; to escape text for pages see <a href="/std/encoding/html/">encoding.html</a>.</p>
>>

doc.Make = Turns a title into a slug with DefaultOptions: transliterated to ASCII, lowercase, words joined by a hyphen, symbols spelled as words, emoji dropped. Free the returned text.
doc.MakeWith = Turns a title into a slug with the options you give. Free the returned text.
doc.Transliterate = Writes a text in ASCII letters and leaves everything else as it is: case, spaces and punctuation stay, digits of other scripts become 0 to 9, emoji and symbols without a spelling become spaces. The language is a code such as fa, ar, ru, uk, bg, sr, de, da or tr; an empty one is detected from the script. Free the returned text.
doc.DetectScript = Names the script most letters of a text belong to: latin, cyrillic, greek, arabic, hebrew, devanagari, thai, japanese, korean or han. Returns an empty text when there are no letters. Free the returned text.
doc.Unique = Returns the slug when the function says it is free, else the first of slug-2, slug-3 and so on that is free. Free the returned text.
doc.UniqueWith = Like Unique with the separator you choose. After ten thousand tries it returns the number reached.
doc.Numbered = Returns the slug followed by the separator and a number, or the slug alone when the number is 1 or less.
doc.IsSlug = Tells whether a text is a slug the default options could produce: lowercase ASCII letters and digits, single hyphens, none at the ends, not empty.
doc.IsSlugWith = Tells whether a text is a slug for the given options: separator, case, allowed characters, native letters, length limit and identifier rule.
doc.DefaultOptions = The usual choice: hyphen separator, lowercase, no length limit, language detected, symbols spelled as words, separators trimmed and merged.
doc.URL = DefaultOptions with the length limited to 100 characters.
doc.Filename = For file names: underscore separator, case kept, dots and hyphens allowed, 200 characters at most, never starts or ends with a dot, falls back to file.
doc.Snake = DefaultOptions with an underscore separator.
doc.Identifier = For names in code: underscore separator, lowercase, never starts with a digit (an underscore is put in front), falls back to an underscore.
doc.Unicode = DefaultOptions that keeps the native letters of every script instead of transliterating.
doc.AddStopWords = Adds words, separated by spaces or commas, that are left out of slugs. They are compared after transliteration and ignoring case.
doc.AddReplacement = Adds a replacement: every occurrence of the pattern in the title is replaced by the value before anything else happens. Patterns are tried in the order they were added.
doc.FreeOptions = Releases the stop word and replacement lists of an Options. Copies share them, so free once.
doc.Options = Everything that decides how a slug is made. Start from DefaultOptions or a preset and change single fields.
doc.Options.separator = Text put between words, a hyphen by default. May be longer than one character or empty.
doc.Options.lowercase = Fold letters to lowercase. On by default.
doc.Options.max_length = Largest length in characters, cut between words. 0 means no limit.
doc.Options.lang = Language code that picks between conventions (de, da, no, uk, bg, sr, fa, ar, ru and so on). Empty means detect it from the script. Region suffixes such as pt-BR are ignored.
doc.Options.keep_unicode = Keep the letters of every script instead of transliterating them.
doc.Options.keep_han = Keep Han characters, which have no table. On by default; when off they are dropped.
doc.Options.stop_words = Words left out of the slug. Use AddStopWords to fill it.
doc.Options.replacements = Pairs of pattern and value applied to the title first. Use AddReplacement to fill it.
doc.Options.trim = Remove separators, and allowed punctuation, from both ends. On by default.
doc.Options.collapse = Merge a run of separators into one. On by default; when off every character that is not kept gives its own separator.
doc.Options.allow = ASCII punctuation that stays in the slug, such as ._~ . A separator is not put next to an allowed character.
doc.Options.symbols = Spell & @ % + $ and currency signs as words. On by default; when off they are only separators.
doc.Options.identifier = Make the result usable as a name in code: when it starts with a digit an underscore is put in front.
doc.Options.fallback = Returned when the slug would be empty.
