pkg.intro <<
<p><code>gui.device</code> reaches the features of a phone that a page cannot draw: the share sheet and more as they are added. Every call has the same signature on every system. On a desktop, or when the phone does not offer the feature, the call returns false or an empty value and the program carries on.</p>
<p><code>Platform</code> names the system the program runs on: <code>android</code>, <code>ios</code> or <code>none</code> on a desktop. <code>CanShare</code> tells whether a share sheet exists, and <code>Share</code> opens it with a text and a link. Either may be empty, but not both. The call returns once the sheet is shown; it does not tell what the user chose.</p>
<p>Calls go through the host layer of the phone shell, so they work in apps built with <code>tools/bash/build-android-app.sh</code>.</p>

<h2 id="guide-share">Share a link</h2>
<p>Check <code>CanShare</code> first when the app also runs on a desktop, and hide the button or fall back to the clipboard when it is false.</p>
{{example.gui-device-Share}}
>>
