pkg.intro <<
<p>In <code>f64</code>, 0.1 + 0.2 is not exactly 0.3. For science and graphics that tiny error does not matter; for money it does, because a lost cent in the accounts is not acceptable. The <code>decimal</code> package keeps a number as a large whole number and a "scale", the count of digits after the point, so 10.05 is 1005 with scale 2 and no error can creep in.</p>
<p>Addition, subtraction and multiplication are always exact. Division may not terminate, as one third does not, so you say how many decimal places you want and how to round. <code>Rounding</code> offers seven methods; accounting usually wants <code>HalfEven</code>.</p>
>>
