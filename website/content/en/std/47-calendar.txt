pkg.intro <<
<p>This package works with three calendars: <strong>Jalali</strong> (Solar Hijri, the official calendar of Iran and Afghanistan), <strong>Hijri</strong> (lunar) and <strong>Gregorian</strong>. A date is a plain struct of year, month and day, and each calendar has its own conversion, validation, day arithmetic and month name functions.</p>
<p>The Hijri calendar here is <em>arithmetic</em>: months alternate between 30 and 29 days and are not adjusted by sighting the moon, so a date can be one day off from the official calendar of a given country.</p>
<p>There are three ways to turn an instant into a date: <code>EpochToJalali</code> and its siblings use the machine local time, <code>WallClockJalali</code> takes a fixed time zone such as Tehran, and <code>LoadZone</code> covers zones that observe daylight saving.</p>
>>
