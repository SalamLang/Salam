pkg.description = The encoding/binary package in Salam: writing and reading 8 to 64 bit integers and floats in a byte buffer, big-endian and little-endian, with an example.
pkg.import = encoding.binary
pkg.intro <<
<p>Binary file formats and network protocols store a number as several bytes in a row. <strong>Big-endian</strong> writes the most significant byte first and is the usual order in network protocols; <strong>little-endian</strong> is the other way round and is what most processors use, and formats such as ZIP.</p>
<p>The writing functions take a <code>ByteBuf</code> and the reading functions take a <code>ByteReader</code>, both from the <a href="../compress/">compress</a> package.</p>
>>
