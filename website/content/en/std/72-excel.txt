pkg.intro <<
<p>A <strong>workbook</strong> holds several <strong>sheets</strong>, and each sheet holds cells by row and column, from zero. Turn Excel references such as <code>C5</code> into a row and column with <code>CellRef</code> and <code>ParseCellRef</code>.</p>
<p><code>Open</code> and <code>Save</code> work the format out from the file extension. Formulas are stored and read with their last calculated result, but Salam does not evaluate a formula itself.</p>
>>
